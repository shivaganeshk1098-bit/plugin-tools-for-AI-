# Explain it simply

Make a complex or unfamiliar topic genuinely easy to understand.

## Steps

1. **Gauge the audience.** Complete beginner, or someone with some background? If
   unclear, aim for "smart person new to this topic" and avoid jargon.
2. **Start with the core idea in one plain sentence** — no jargon, no caveats yet.
3. **Build understanding:**
   - Use a concrete **analogy** or everyday example to anchor the idea.
   - Explain the **why** — what problem it solves or why it matters.
   - Add detail in small, ordered steps, defining any necessary terms as you go.
4. **Check the mental model.** End with a quick recap or a simple example that lets
   the reader confirm they got it.

## Style

- Short sentences, everyday words. Expand jargon the first time you must use it.
- Analogies should clarify, not mislead — note where an analogy breaks down if it
  matters.
- Don't oversimplify to the point of being wrong; keep it true, just accessible.

## Notes

- Depth is a dial: offer to go deeper ("want the more technical version?") rather
  than dumping everything at once.
