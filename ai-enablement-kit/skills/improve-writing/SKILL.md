# Improve writing

Make the user's writing clearer and stronger while keeping their meaning and voice.

## Steps

1. **Read for intent.** Understand what the author means and who it's for. You're
   editing, not rewriting into something generic.
2. **Edit in layers:**
   - **Correctness** — grammar, spelling, punctuation.
   - **Clarity** — remove ambiguity; one clear idea per sentence.
   - **Concision** — cut filler, redundancy, and empty hedging.
   - **Tone** — shift toward the requested register (professional, friendly,
     confident) only if asked.
   - **Flow** — logical order and smooth transitions.
3. **Preserve the voice.** Keep the author's personality and good phrasing; don't
   flatten it into corporate boilerplate.

## Output

- **Improved version** — ready to use.
- **Key changes** — a short list of notable edits and why (so the author learns).
- **(Optional)** a more formal or more casual variant if useful.

## Notes

- Improve, don't homogenize — the goal is the author's message said better.
- Flag anything unclear or a claim that seems off, rather than silently changing
  meaning.
- For longer or higher-stakes pieces, consider the `editor` agent.
