# Meeting notes → structured recap

Turn messy notes or a transcript into a clear, shareable summary.

## Steps

1. **Read it all** to understand what the meeting was about and what was decided.
2. **Extract the structure:**
   - **Summary** — 2-4 sentences on what the meeting covered and concluded.
   - **Decisions** — what was actually agreed (not just discussed).
   - **Action items** — each as `owner → task → due date` (mark owner/date
     `[unassigned]` / `[no date]` if not stated; don't invent them).
   - **Open questions** — unresolved items needing follow-up.
3. **Separate signal from chatter.** Keep what matters for someone who missed the
   meeting; drop small talk and tangents.
4. **Stay faithful.** Only capture what's actually in the notes. If something is
   ambiguous, flag it rather than guessing who owns it.

## Output

A clean recap with the sections above, formatted so it can be pasted straight
into an email, doc, or chat.

## Notes

- Action items are the most valuable output — make them specific and owned.
- Don't attribute decisions or tasks to people unless the notes clearly do.
